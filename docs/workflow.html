VD Booth: workflow of the 2 Figma prototypes

The reviewed screen-by-screen workflow for the three journeys SEND / RECEIVE / COLLECT, built from 24 Figma frames: what each screen does in business terms, where the visitor taps to move on, what moves, which screens scroll, and where the design has gaps. The build in this repo follows this page. Decisions are recorded in docs/DECISIONS.md and verification results are in docs/verify/report.html.

General principles

100% faithful to the prototype

  • Each Figma frame is one step. The step counts are kept: SEND 10, RECEIVE 6, COLLECT 8.
  • Copy, amounts, exchange rates, reference codes and status-bar times are taken verbatim from the JSON. Nothing is translated, re-rounded or "fixed".
  • English only, including the kiosk shell. The VI/EN switch is removed.
  • Every disclaimer line is kept ("Illustrative scenario…", "VXBS concept…").

Navigation inside the phone

  • One hotspot per screen: the Figma element that acts as the CTA, listed in each screen’s "Forward" row. The hotspot gets a soft pulsing ring after 4 seconds without a touch.
  • The Back button (left arrow) returns to the previous step. Other elements (tab bar, quick actions, dropdowns) only give touch feedback and do not change the screen.
  • Screen transitions: a 320 ms horizontal slide like an iOS push; Back slides the other way.
  • Status screens (review, tracking) play their timeline on their own. A screen with no CTA advances automatically.

Scrolling and the scroll hint

  • The phone frame is 402 × 874 (iPhone 16 Pro). Content taller than the frame scrolls inside it. The header/navigation and footer CTA stay fixed, as in Figma.
  • Bottom scroll hint: a fade plus a gently bouncing chevron above the footer. It hides once the bottom is reached and shows again on scrolling up.
  • When the CTA sits inside the scrolling content (RECEIVE: Receive funds), the hint reads "Scroll to continue" until the CTA is in view.
  • COLLECT fits the 874 frame and does not scroll.

Unchanged from the previous demo

  • 16:9 1920 × 1080 stage, particle map background, ?zone= ?idle= ?reload= ?fx= ?qr=.
  • Runs offline (vendor/), reloads itself every 2 hours, keeps the screen awake, fx=low.
  • Icons are the SVGs exported from Figma (figma/assets/), kept in the repo; nothing is loaded from the network.
  • The previous demo (GitHub Pages and vd-booth-demo.vercel.app) is untouched. This rebuild lives only on branch figma-wip + Vercel project vd-booth-demo-wip.

The layout follows the previous demo: the left column holds the journey name, the headline and the step list, the phone sits centre-right, and the "route" card is in the bottom-right corner. All shell copy is in English. The step list on the left uses the Figma frame names.

Implementation notes

Full detail in docs/IMPL-PLAN.md.

Structure

  • A dev-time generator, scripts/gen/build-screens.mjs (pure Node, no dependencies), compiles figma/rest/*.json into screens/gen/screens.js + screens/gen/screens.css. The output is committed and never edited by hand; re-run node scripts/gen/build-screens.mjs after a Figma pull.
  • One element per Figma node, each tagged with data-node (RECEIVE also data-twin). Sizes, spacing, radii, colours and line heights come from the REST JSON, not from eyeballing. Per-node patches live in scripts/gen/overrides.json, each with a reason.
  • Icons come from figma/assets/<fileKey>/*.svg and are inlined by the generator.
  • screens/behaviors.js adds the hotspots, Back, tabs, auto-advance and the motion listed on this page. Motion starts from a temporary state and always ends on the generated Figma state. screens/phone.css holds the phone host, kiosk/solo modes, scroll hint and hotspot ring. index.html / app.js are the English-only kiosk shell; full Inter is in vendor/.

Verification

  • The harness in scripts/verify/ (puppeteer-core with the local Chrome, network blocked) runs with pnpm --dir scripts/verify verify. The exit code is non-zero on any hard failure.
  • Each screen is rendered alone with index.html?solo=<zone>-<n> and checked against the Figma REST JSON and the reference PNGs in figma/screens/: text, geometry, style and pixel layers.
  • An end-to-end flow run at 1920 × 1080 walks every journey: forward CTAs, Back, inert elements, scroll hint, the Done QR, idle return, English only, offline, ?fx=low.
  • Results: docs/verify/report.html and docs/verify/report.json. Deploy: figma-wip → Vercel project vd-booth-demo-wip; main, gh-pages and vd-booth-demo.vercel.app are not touched.
